SkyHanni Support Bot Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data SkyHanni Support Bot ("the bot") processes and how. The bot is operated by the SkyHanni project and its source code is public at https://github.com/SkyHanniStudios/DiscordBot.

1. Data processed in memory. To function, the bot reads the content of messages in servers it is in, so it can detect commands prefixed with "!", inline pull-request references such as #123, posted mod lists, and Discord invite links. This message content is processed in memory only and is discarded immediately after processing. The bot does not store regular users' message content and does not build user profiles.
2. Data stored outside Discord. The only data persisted off-platform is staff-authored bot configuration, namely the canned tag responses that staff create, stored in a local database, and diagnostic logs containing user identifiers (user ID, username, nickname), the channel name, and a short description of the action performed. These logs may contain staff-authored text such as a tag response or a moderation reason, but never the message content of regular users.
3. What we do not do. We do not sell your data, share it with third parties, or use any data to train machine learning or AI models. We do not store regular users' message content outside Discord.
4. Discord. The bot operates within Discord and is subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy. Some output, such as a flagged unknown invite link, is posted into a staff channel and therefore stays within Discord.
5. Retention and deletion. Diagnostic logs are kept only as long as needed for operation and troubleshooting. You may request deletion of data relating to you by contacting the SkyHanni staff on the SkyHanni Support Server (https://discord.gg/skyhanni).
6. Your rights. Depending on your jurisdiction, for example under the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Use the contact methods above to exercise them.
7. Changes. We may update this Policy from time to time; the "last updated" date reflects the latest version.
8. Contact. SkyHanni Support Server (https://discord.gg/skyhanni).
